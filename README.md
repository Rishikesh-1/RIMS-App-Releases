## 📋 Installation Guide (Android)

1. Click the download link above on your Android phone or scan the QR code from the school website.
2. Once the download finishes, tap the downloaded `.apk` file from your browser downloads or notification bar.
3. If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   - Tap **Settings**.
   - Toggle **Allow from this source** to ON.
   - Tap **Back** and select **Install**.
4. Launch **Alchemist Academy / RIMS** from your app drawer and log in with your student, parent, or staff credentials.
